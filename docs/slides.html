The right_join is the exact opposite:

So Which One?

Q: So which one should I actually use?

A: No easy answer. Depends on the treatment of missing values.

  • inner_join() drops missing values, simplifying later steps

  • outer joins (left, right, full) force you to interact with missing values

Think about what you will do with missing values.

  • Report number of missing values: left_join
  • Discard and carry on: inner_join

Missingness

Three models of missingness:

  • Missing Completely at Random (MCAR) - Random & Unrelated to QoIs
    • Data Termites
  • Missing at Random (MAR) - Random, but Correlated to QoIs
  • Missing Not at Random (MNAR) - Adversarial
    • Data Demons

MCAR missingness is a strong and rare condition, but it’s really the only situation where it’s totally safe to just drop missing values.

Why is it missing?

  • flights data: missing delays indicate cancelled flights. Should you throw them out when looking at on time performance?
  • weather data: data missing when tornado blows monitor off the roof. Impact on average wind speed?
